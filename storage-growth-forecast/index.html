Storage Growth Forecast

Fit linear and compounding growth to usage history and find the date you run out of space.

Usage history

Two columns: date (YYYY-MM-DD) and used space. A header row is fine. Snapshots can be daily, weekly or monthly.

Assumptions

Projected capacity use

Example data
Actual Linear trend Compounding trend Capacity

Scenarios

Linear adds a fixed amount each month. Compounding grows by a fixed percentage each month, so it reaches capacity sooner when growth is accelerating. Both are least-squares fits to your history. Treat the gap between them as your planning range.
