# Geoffer López — Portfolio Site

A single-page portfolio built from your CV, aimed at recruiters and hiring managers evaluating
you for DBA, cloud, and Solutions Architect / Forward Deployed Engineer roles.

Plain HTML/CSS/JS — no build step, no dependencies. Just three files: index.html, style.css,
script.js.

## Before you publish

Open index.html and search for REPLACE_WITH_EMAIL@example.com (it appears twice, hero and
contact section) and swap in your real email address.

## What's in the page

Hero with a career status panel surfacing headline metrics (26 yrs in IT, 20 as a DBA,
sub-5-minute recovery time, the 3-hour-to-5-minute query tuning win, 300+ servers, 4-time-zone
follow-the-sun coverage), professional summary, a dedicated Cloud and Continuous Learning
section, technical expertise grouped by platform, full reverse-chronological experience,
education, certifications, languages, and contact.

All content comes directly from the resume provided, nothing was invented or embellished.
